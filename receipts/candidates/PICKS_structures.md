- tower_house: c3 (stone ground storey with quoins, plaster shaft, open arched loggia under a hip roof), 2752 tris, 5.37 x 11.54 x 5.38 m. Loggia gives a readable crown at 100 m and dark openings on all four sides. c1 (2112, coursed base + cone roof) faithful to o01 but bland, arched door read as a grate; c2 (1460, 4-segment lathes) clean but the thinnest detail, tower reads as a plain box.
- chapel: c1 (box nave + barrel-tile gable roof, square base + drum + hemisphere dome, open round-arched belfry on the front gable, apse with half dome, quoins), 5610 tris, 8.55 x 13.5 x 14.45 m. Closest to o05, belfry empty, no cross. c2 (3084, extruded/lathed) similar massing but the stepped-course apse roof rendered as odd stripes and the roof reads flat; c3 (7920, cross plan + octagonal drum) handsome but wider than the brief (10.9 m) and its bell-cote is squat and reads as a chimney.
- clock_tower: c3 (rusticated stone shaft built course by course, blank ring clock faces in proud square frames on all four sides, paired open arches per face, bracketed eaves, hip roof), 7164 tris, 5.58 x 18.06 x 5.63 m. Strongest stone read and clock face reads at distance; no numerals, no hands. c1 (3684, primitives) good but stone shaft plain; c2 (3608, lathe shaft) clock ring too faint to read, banding barely visible.
- water_mill: c1 (primitives), 3442 tris. Closest to o02: stone ground storey with chalk quoins, king-post half-timbered limewash upper storey with teal/madder windows, dark bargeboards, undershot wheel in a stone race with a bearing pier; 9.3 m tall, all four sides modelled. joints.wheel pivot = axle (checked: bbox centre unchanged after a 45 deg turn about X). c2 (extruded walls with real holes, 4286) was equally clean but roof stripes read as flat planks and it ran 9.8 m; c3 (overshot wheel + flume on trestles, X-braced jetty, 3644) had the most character but is a different mill from the reference and the flume pushed depth to 11.1 m. Note: water strip is shutter-teal with no material name (no contract name fits water).
- windmill: c3 (faceted tower, ring-laid chunky stone base, radial-plank cap, ladder sails with a diagonal tied cloth + rolled edge), 2276 tris. Only one whose sails read like o04 (lattice visible, half-wrapped cloth) and whose base reads as stone blocks. joints.sails pivot = hub (checked about Z). c1 (cylinder/cone, full trapezoid cloths, 1996) read as solid white paddles, no lattice; c2 (single lathe tower, ogee cap, extruded grid lattice, 3564) good tower but cloth panels again hid the lattice and the ogee cap reads Dutch rather than Mediterranean. Includes a tail pole to the back (not in the ref) which sets depth to 9.4 m.
- quarry_crane: c3 (twin-beam jib with spacers, sill frame, raking back legs, winch with two 2.8 m hand wheels), 1828 tris, after one revision to chunkier sections (mast 0.8 m, jib 0.46 m beams). Twin jib and big winch match o04. c1 (primitives, 984) and c2 (chamfered swept timbers, sagging tube ropes, lathed drum, 2648) were both too spindly at 12 m and their winch was undersized; c2's sagging stay was the nicest single detail.
- stone_bridge: c4 (refined c1, profile arm), 2516 tris. Whole body one extruded side profile with a 9 m segmental arch cut out, deck top sampled on 0.4 + 2.0*cos(pi*x/18); proud voussoir ring, string course, segmented chalk coping, end piers outside the walkway. Deck check (scratch/deckcheck.mjs, walkway |z|<1.79): max deviation 0.002 m, 0 samples above the line. Others: c1 same build but arch small and coping overhung the walkway (sampled 1.2 m above line at the piers); c2 tilted-slab chain had a striped picket look under the deck and piers inside the walkway; c3 tiny arch, odd cutwaters and oculi, splayed wings made it 6.5 m deep.
- stone_steps: c2 (profile arm), 1132 tris. One extruded staircase profile, bullnosed tread stones, sloping cheek walls with chalk coping and newel blocks; reads cleanly as a stair at distance and from every side. Others: c1 stepped cheek walls and tread slabs too noisy, read as rubble; c3 pillow stones closest to the painting but 9836 tris (over the 4k prop budget) and a blank back.
- terrace_wall: c2 (rubble reading), 2588 tris. Each face laid separately with flattened icosahedron boulders over a core; bulging stones shade like dry-stone, matches the ref best. Others: c1 through-box stones read as brick coursing; c3 extruded irregular polygons were flat-faced, joints only read up close.
- arcade_section: c1 (profile arm), 3200 tris. - market_stall: c2 (profiles), 2744 tris. Bowed extruded awning stripes with scalloped valance read like the ref; closed planked counter reads from front AND back; baskets of fruit plus a crate. c1 (primitives, 2892) flat awning and gappy alternating front planks read as bars; c3 (trestle + back wall, 4994) over the 4k prop budget and its back is one blank plank wall.
- stone_well: c1 (primitives, revised), 1860 tris. Staggered coursed block ring plus extruded ring-sector cap slabs match the ref; dark frame with braces, rope coil, banded bucket.
