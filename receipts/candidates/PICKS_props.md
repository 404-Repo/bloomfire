# BLOOMFIRE props and vegetation picks (path B, 3 candidates each)

Candidates are built from bodies in work/assets_props_scratch/src by gen.mjs (adds identical helpers + the contract placement block; seed fixed per object/file so output is deterministic). Verified with harness/verify.mjs per object; winners re-verified together in work/assets_props_scratch/winners/_verify/sheet.png (13/15 clean, 2 known warnings below). Budgets are the brief's (hundreds placed). No decimation anywhere; budgets met with low segment counts.

- olive_tree: c2 (sweeps, revised: trunk thicker, masses lifted to 4.6 m), 966 tris. Two tapered tube strands twisting round each other, swept limbs to five masses of three dodecahedra in the two olive greens. Only one with o08's twisted trunk and separate clumps. c1 (786, split cylinder trunk + 7 icosphere clumps) read as a generic broadleaf; c3 (680, broad umbrella) closer to the concept paintings' distant trees but its bole looks like an oak/acacia.
- cypress: c1 (lathe flame profile with lumpy noise and twist), 312 tris. Widest a third up, pointed, matches o08. Verifier "back face featureless" is a lighting artefact on a rotationally symmetric column (the same warning fired on c2); the back is modelled. c2 (438, icosahedron tufts) too slender and clumpy at the base; c3 (318, warped cylinder) widest at the base, reads as a cone.
- lemon_tree_pot: c1 (primitives, lemons enlarged to 9.5 cm), 606 tris. Round topiary ball with lemons studded round it, banded pot: closest to o08. c2 (894) and c3 (766) multi-mass canopies read as a generic shrub and their fruit hid inside the masses.
- lavender_bush: c3 (hand-built, revised twice: wider splay, small leaf core added), 304 tris. Folded grey-green blades + 28 stems with bipyramid spikes; spikes over the whole crown like o08. c1 (376) mound read as a rock with spikes bunched to one side; c2 (208) blob mound + cones read as a rock with a purple crest.
- flower_patch: c1 (primitives, heads enlarged to 16-18 cm), 237 tris. Flat 5/6-petal discs with contrasting centres (poppy red, white, ochre) + lavender spikes over grass blades; the most cheerful, colour reads as a mass at 10 m. c2 (290, octahedron heads) heads looked like confetti chips; c3 (238) its green mound disc will fight the terrain and it ran 0.45 m tall.
- grass_tuft: c1 (hand-built folded blades), 60 tris. Verifier flags "only 60 triangles" (its floor is 150); this is the brief's under-80 budget, accepted. c2 (66, 22 cones) equal look at 11x the meshes pre-merge; c3 (54) bent strips formed a cup.
- boulder: c2 (icosphere sliced by 11 planes, widened, two chips), 152 tris, 3.1 x 1.84 x 2.2 m. Broad flat fracture faces read as limestone, not a sphere. c1 (152, noise only) a lumpy potato; c3 (96, bedded slabs) read as a man-made stack.
- cart: c1 (primitives), 948 tris. Ochre bed, plank sides with stakes, straight shafts to the ground at +Z, 8-spoke wheels with iron tyres: o06 match from all sides. c2 (1524, extruded panels, curved shafts) sides read as solid slabs; c3 (1096, pitched at rest, slatted) nice but busier, slats noisy at distance.
- barrel_cluster: c2 (lathes), 1084 tris. Standing barrel, crate with X faces, second barrel lying chocked on it (gives the 1.25 m), lathed amphora in front: o07 arrangement. c1 (1140) same layout but its crate brace stuck out of the side and thin hoops vanished; c3 (1076, crate on a barrel) reads as clutter, not o07.
- crate_stack: c3 (profile: each face one extruded braced panel), 696 tris. Chunky frame + corner-to-corner brace like o07, braces on lids too. c1 (612, edge boards + diagonals) close second, slightly busier; c2 (720, slatted) reads as fruit crates, not o07.
- amphora: c2 (lathe body + tube handles + ring stand), 630 tris. Cleanest classic silhouette with pointed toe in a three-legged ring stand. c1 (646) torus handles sat awkwardly; c3 (460, square stand) chunky, reads as a pot on a stool.
- wooden_fence: c1 (primitives), 252 tris. Three square posts with pyramid caps, two rails, iron bolt heads: o07. c2 (180, log posts, hewn rails) too thin at distance; c3 (312, braced) nice but the brace makes it a gate.
- haystack: c1 (lathed beehive + ragged tuft skirt + pole), 312 tris. Shaggy dome closest to o07. c2 (560, stacked tori) read as a beehive/cake; c3 (336, icosphere) a lumpy ball with floating ground patches.
- field_wall: c2 (profile, revised: 5-sided stones, random rotation, fewer stones; was 2000 tris), 1372 tris, 5.13 x 1.06 x 0.56 m. Battered core faced both sides with irregular extruded stones and rounded coping: reads as dry stone. c1 (1296, through-box courses) read as brick coursing; c3 (1524, dodecahedron rubble) 0.62 m deep and coping overhang.
- chalk_block: c2 (beveled extruded chipped outline + raised tool-mark bands on all four faces + spall chip), 208 tris. Chip reads at distance, softened quarry edges. c1 (216, grooved box) grooves rendered as zigzag triangle shading; c3 (144) read as a layered stack.
